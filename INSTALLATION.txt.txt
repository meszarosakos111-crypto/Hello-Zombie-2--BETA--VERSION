INSTALLATION

1. Win and R %appdata%
2. .minecraft
3. Put the map folder into:
.minecraft/saves

4. Put the resource pack into:
.minecraft/resourcepacks

5. Enable the resource pack.
6. Start the world.

Recommended:
- Render Distance: 21 chunks
- Sound: 100%
- Brightness: Moody
-Fov:60

Make sure,that its realistic,and Horror.
