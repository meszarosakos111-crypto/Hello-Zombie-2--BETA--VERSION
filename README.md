The Neighbor: Horror Adventure
About

The Neighbor is a Minecraft horror adventure map where you explore a mysterious house, discover secrets, solve puzzles, and try to escape.

Something is wrong with the Neighbor...
He is hiding something inside his house.

Features
Horror atmosphere
Custom events and jumpscares
The Neighbor AI system
Hidden rooms and secrets
Glitch and mystery effects
Story-based gameplay
Objective

Your mission:

Enter the Neighbor's house
Find hidden keys
Discover the secret basement
Avoid the Neighbor
Escape alive
Rules
Do not break the map
Do not cheat
Play in Survival Mode
Use sounds for the best experience
Follow the objectives

Tips:
Stay quiet
Hide when the Neighbor is nearby
Search every room
Watch for strange events
Do not trust everything you see
